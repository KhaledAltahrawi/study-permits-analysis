The Shift in Canadian Study Permits

A 10-Year Data Journey (2015 – 2025)

Chapter 1: The Decade of Growth

Between 2015 and 2024, Canada saw a historic rise in international students. This chart shows the momentum we've had, peaking just before the recent policy shifts took hold.

The Insight: Look at the 2021 rebound—after the pandemic "dip," Canada opened its doors wider than ever before.

Chapter 2: Where is everyone coming from?

Immigration isn't just numbers; it's about connections to the world. A few specific countries have become the backbone of the student community here.

The Insight: India, China, and Nigeria dominate the list. Interestingly, the trends for these top countries often move differently based on specific regional events.

Chapter 3: The "August Rush"

Ever wonder why August feels so busy in university towns? The data proves it. Permit issuance follows a strict "heartbeat" every year.

The Insight: There is a massive spike every late summer. If you're looking for a permit outside of this window, you're in the minority!

Chapter 4: Are things slowing down?

This is the part everyone is talking about. By looking at the Year-over-Year (YoY) change, we can see if the new caps are actually working.

The Insight: The red lines show the "cooldown." While permits are still being issued, the rate of growth has shifted dramatically compared to the "boom" years.
