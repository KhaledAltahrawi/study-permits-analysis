Chapter 1: The Decade of Growth & The 2023 Peak

Between 2015 and 2023, Canada experienced an unprecedented surge in international student enrollment. Following the 2020 pandemic dip (~271K), study permit issuance grew at a record-breaking pace, culminating in an all-time peak of 700,395 permits in 2023.

Key Insight: The post-pandemic acceleration in 2021–2023 caused nationwide volumes to nearly triple from 2015 baseline levels, creating acute housing and community infrastructure challenges that triggered decisive federal policy intervention.

Chapter 2: Top 10 Source Countries

Over the 2015–2026 period, international student intake has been heavily concentrated in a small group of key partner nations. India and China account for the vast majority of cumulative permit holders.

Key Insight: India represents over 1.5 million cumulative permits—more than the next five countries combined—making national trends acutely sensitive to changes in Indian student mobility.

Chapter 4: The "August Rush" & Seasonality

Permit issuance strictly adheres to the Canadian academic intake calendar. Late summer (August and September) consistently accounts for the highest annual volume as universities and colleges prepare for Fall matriculation.

Key Insight: August alone accounts for nearly double the permit volume of average months, whereas February and March represent historical low periods for new issuances.

Chapter 5: Measuring the Policy Cap Impact (2024 – 2026)

In early 2024, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) introduced an intake cap and provincial attestation letters (PALs) to stabilize international student growth. The data reveals the decisive impact of these measures:

  • 2024: Total permits dropped -24.2% (from 700K to 530K) following the initial cap implementation.
  • 2025: Contraction continued with another -24.5% decline down to 400K as full compliance took hold.
  • 2026 (YTD): Issuance volumes reached 273,805 through September, reflecting the ongoing stabilized baseline following the federal intake caps.
Key Finding: The empirical data confirms that the federal policy caps successfully reversed the decade-long growth spiral, reducing new annual student permit issuances by over 42% from the 2023 peak and establishing a stable new intake baseline for 2026.
